      We spread a little Christmas cheer at [The Whitaker](https://thewhitaker.org/) - Rossendale's museum and art gallery in Rawtenstall - performing on Sunday 1st December as part of their Winterfest event.

      ## A Merry Gathering

      Between 2:45 and 3:45 in the restaurant area we played Christmas music and other hits for a merry room of visitors, amid Winterfest's craft stalls, grotto and festive family fun. Thanks to everyone at The Whitaker for having us - it was a lovely way to round off our first full year of sessions and performances.

      The Whitaker is a registered charity transformed into a winter wonderland each December - if you're in the Rossendale area, it's well worth a visit when Winterfest comes round again.
