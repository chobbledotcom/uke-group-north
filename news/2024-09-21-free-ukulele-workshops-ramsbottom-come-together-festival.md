      We held free ukulele workshops at Ramsbottom Royal British Legion as part of the [Ramsbottom Come Together Festival](https://rctf.co.uk/) - a free community arts and culture festival celebrating music, heritage and togetherness across a dozen venues in Ramsbottom.

      ## Something for Every Level

      The workshops were designed to work as a genuine first taste for beginners curious about picking up an instrument, while also covering techniques and skills for more advanced players. Nobody needed to bring anything - we brought a box of ukuleles to borrow.

      That's the model for all our community workshops: we come to you, instruments and all. If you'd like us to run one for your group, school or workplace, email [ukegroupnorth@gmail.com](mailto:ukegroupnorth@gmail.com).
