      Winterfest invited us back, and we said yes. On Sunday 7th December we returned to [The Whitaker](https://thewhitaker.org/) in Rawtenstall for their Winterfest weekend - tinsel up, songbooks out, festive jumpers worn with pride.

      A big thanks to everyone at The Whitaker and to everyone who came along to listen and sing. A wonderful event - we really enjoyed ourselves, and playing the same stage two years running is becoming a rather nice Christmas tradition of our own.

      If you'd like live ukulele at your festive event - or any event - email [ukegroupnorth@gmail.com](mailto:ukegroupnorth@gmail.com).
