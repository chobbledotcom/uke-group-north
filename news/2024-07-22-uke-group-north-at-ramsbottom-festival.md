      We spent the weekend at [Ramsbottom Festival](https://www.burytimes.co.uk/news/24454404.ramsbottom-festival-coming-town-weekend/) - the first food and drink festival organised by Ramsbottom Rotary Club, running across the town on 20th and 21st July with a fairground, bandstand events, a classic car show, craft stalls and more.

      ## A Joyful Weekend

      We set up alongside the brilliant Rossendale Ukulele Club, and the best part was watching festival-goers stop, listen, and then pick up a ukulele and join in - exactly the spirit our sessions run on. Thank you to everyone who came by to check out what we do, and particularly to those who played some music with us!

      If you caught us at the festival and fancy doing it properly, our weekly Sing & Strum sessions are the place to start - see our [events page](/events/) or email [ukegroupnorth@gmail.com](mailto:ukegroupnorth@gmail.com).
