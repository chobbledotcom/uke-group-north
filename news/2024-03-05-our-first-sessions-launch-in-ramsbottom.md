      This week we launched our very first Sing & Strum sessions - two Tuesday groups in Ramsbottom, run by professional musicians and open to absolutely everyone.

      ## Where and When

      **Ramsbottom Library, Carr Street**
      Tuesdays, 10:30am-12pm - £8 per person

      **The Den, Stubbins Vale Road (with Rammy Men)**
      Tuesdays, 5:30pm-7pm - £6 per person

      No musical experience is necessary, sessions are inclusive of all abilities, and if you don't own a ukulele you can hire one of ours for the session for an additional £2 - just let us know in advance.

      ## A Home at The Den

      Our evening sessions are hosted at The Den, the community space run by [Rammy Men](https://rammymen.org) at Stubbins Vale Mill. Rammy Men was set up to support men's mental health in Ramsbottom and Bury after the town lost local men to suicide, and it has grown into a community organisation involving hundreds of people in activities every week. We're proud to be part of that programme - you can read about their work and their other activities, from Thai Chi to walking groups, on their website.

      ## Join Us

      Spaces are limited, so email [ukegroupnorth@gmail.com](mailto:ukegroupnorth@gmail.com) to reserve your seat and instrument.
