      We recently visited [The Met](https://themet.org.uk/) in Bury to deliver our Introduction to Ukulele workshop for the [Meet Me at The Met](https://themet.org.uk/event/meet-me-at-the-met/) community group - and it was wonderfully well received. Thank you to everyone who took part!

      ## About the Group

      Meet Me at The Met is a social arts group for anyone aged 50 and over, meeting fortnightly on Monday mornings at The Met - Bury's award-winning live music venue and theatre, run by the Bury Metropolitan Arts Association charity. From puppetry to textiles, the group tries a bit of everything, and this time it was ukuleles.

      ## The Workshop

      Our two-hour Introduction to Ukulele session is designed for complete beginners and novice players: the best starting advice, foundation techniques, and help ironing out any bad habits before they take hold. Intermediate players get plenty from it too - solid fundamentals are where the good stuff grows from.

      No experience or instrument is required - we bring the ukuleles.

      ## Book Us for Your Group

      If your community group, school, workplace, event or venue would like us to bring over a big box of ukuleles and deliver an Introduction to Ukulele workshop, email [ukegroupnorth@gmail.com](mailto:ukegroupnorth@gmail.com).
