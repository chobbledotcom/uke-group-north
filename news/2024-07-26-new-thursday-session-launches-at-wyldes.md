      Bury town centre has its own Sing & Strum session! We announced the new Thursday group in July, threw open the doors of Wyldes on Bolton Street for a free opening night on Thursday 25th July - and it was, in chord-notation terms, most exc'llent.

      ## Opening Night

      The room was a proper mix: people who had never held a ukulele before sitting alongside professional musicians, everyone singing together with focus points on ukulele practice and technique. That blend is exactly what makes the sessions work.

      ## The Venue

      [Wyldes](https://www.joseph-holt.com/pubs/wyldes) is a cosy Joseph Holt pub in the historic part of Bury town centre - a former bank building, three floors of bar and lounge, a short walk from the Metrolink stop and across the road from The Met. Free parking nearby after 6pm.

      Times have shifted a little since we opened - check our [events page](/events/) for the current schedule, and email [ukegroupnorth@gmail.com](mailto:ukegroupnorth@gmail.com) to book a seat and an instrument.
